- `QoraFlow.euxfel.build_rocking_scan` and `extract_rocking_run` take `frame_filter(images, xgm) -> mask`, applied per scan step and pulse before the sums; the scan records `frames_total` and `frames_kept`. `linear_model_band_mask` returns the mask behind `filter_images_qbpm_by_linear_model`.
- `linear_model_band_mask` keeps every frame when there are fewer than 3 (no fit with an error is possible), and takes `band="residual"` for a band of `sigma` times the residual standard deviation, whose kept fraction does not depend on the number of frames. The default `band="parameter"` is unchanged.
