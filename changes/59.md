- `QoraFlow.euxfel.build_rocking_scans` and `extract_rocking_runs` take `frame_filters={name: filter or None}` and return one `RockingScan` per name from a single read of the frames; `None` gives the unfiltered scan. `build_rocking_scan` and `extract_rocking_run` are unchanged and call them with one filter.
- `linear_model_band_mask(..., band="robust", iterations=3)`: a band of `sigma` times the median absolute deviation of the residuals (times 1.4826), refitted on the kept frames up to `iterations` times. The default `band="parameter"` is unchanged.
